About Grace Farms

Our journey in providing quality poultry and livestock since 2015

Our Story

Grace Farms was founded in 2015 with a simple mission: to provide high-quality, affordable poultry and livestock to families and businesses in Jejeti and the surrounding communities.

What started as a small backyard poultry operation with just 200 layer birds has grown into a thriving farm serving hundreds of customers across the Eastern Region. Through dedication, hard work, and a commitment to excellence, we've expanded our operations to include broilers, goats, sheep, and turkeys.

Today, Grace Farms is recognized as one of the most reliable sources of farm-fresh products in the area, known for our healthy animals, fair prices, and exceptional customer service.

Our Mission

To provide healthy, high-quality poultry and livestock products that nourish families and support businesses, while maintaining sustainable farming practices that respect our environment and community.

Our Vision

To become the leading poultry and livestock farm in Ghana's Eastern Region, setting the standard for quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction in the agricultural sector.
